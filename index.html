About Us

Located in the heart of Aba's fashion district at 2/4 Onyenso Street Off 45 Omenazu by Faulks Road, Dike John Oil & Textiles has established itself as the go-to destination for fashion designers, tailors, and style enthusiasts across Aba North and beyond. We pride ourselves on offering an extensive range of high-quality fabrics sourced both locally and internationally.

Our knowledgeable team understands the unique needs of Nigerian fashion creators and is committed to providing not just materials, but inspiration and support for your creative journey. Whether you're a professional fashion designer working on a collection or an individual looking for that perfect fabric for a special occasion, we have the expertise and inventory to serve you excellently.
