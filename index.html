Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mutual fund?

A mutual fund is an investment vehicle that pools money from multiple investors to purchase a diversified portfolio of stocks, bonds, or other securities, managed by professional fund managers.

What is the minimum investment amount?

Our mutual funds have varying minimum investment amounts starting from ₦50,000, making wealth building accessible to a wide range of investors.

How safe are my investments with Elnods Financial?

We operate under strict regulatory guidelines and employ rigorous risk management practices. All investments are managed with transparency, and regular reports are provided to investors.

How do I start investing?

Simply contact us via phone or WhatsApp, schedule a consultation, complete our client onboarding process, and our team will guide you through selecting the right investment products for your goals.

What returns can I expect?

Returns vary based on market conditions and the type of investment product. We provide realistic projections based on historical performance and current market analysis during your consultation.

Can I withdraw my investment anytime?

Withdrawal terms depend on the specific investment product. Some mutual funds offer liquidity with notice periods, while others have fixed tenures. Our team will explain all terms clearly before you invest.

Do you offer investment advice?

Yes, our experienced underwriters and investment advisors provide comprehensive financial planning and investment advisory services tailored to your unique financial situation.

Are you registered and regulated?

Elnods Financial Mutual Funds Limited operates in compliance with all applicable Nigerian financial regulations and maintains the highest professional standards in the industry.
